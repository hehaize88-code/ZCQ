## Material caveats for editors

1. **Returns are conditional and official text is inconsistent.** The return page's table and fee note disagree about original delivery charges on free-shipping goods. The user agreement describes limited complimentary returns while the dedicated return page lists a service fee. Avoid a universal return-price promise; direct readers to support and the live order screen.
2. **No verified storage-day allowance.** The inspected official pages do not establish a free-storage duration, paid extension rate, disposal deadline or a free-photo count. Do not invent 30/60/90/180 days or 3/5/6 photos.
3. **Do not claim zero service fees.** The user agreement mentions an 8% shipping-related platform fee alongside other service-fee language. This is a dated research observation, not a verified checkout quote. An article should teach users to inspect the complete payment breakdown rather than assert a universal current tariff.
4. **Do not publish legal conclusions from these pages.** The general terms contain inconsistent jurisdiction wording. The official site also contains marketing language broader than its detailed policies. Describe the source and direct policy questions to the platform.
5. **Quality control is limited evidence.** A warehouse image does not establish authenticity, materials, safety, internal function, seller consistency or what a future buyer will receive. Distinguish gallery images, historic community QC and the buyer's own actual order photos.
6. **Do not transplant official promises into this preview.** Pikobuy's official homepage mentions image submissions. That does not mean the independent Pikobuy Sheet image-search route is operational. Its upstream integration remains unconfirmed, and live QC availability is a separate upstream issue.
7. **Payment badges are insufficient for blanket support claims.** No live checkout has been tested. No claim of PayPal support, guaranteed currency rates or fee-free payment is justified.
8. **Support timing is not independently tested.** The guide advertises around-the-clock support. Avoid turning this into a measured response-time promise.
9. **Independent site disclosure.** The logo/brand context should never suggest Pikobuy Sheet is operated by Pikobuy or provides its customer service. Registration and ordering occur at the official domain.
