# Pikobuy keyword and editorial research

Research date: 17 October 2026. Project: pikobuysheet.cc temporary preview. This report is a qualitative search-result and competitor-content review, **not** a search-volume, ranking, traffic or keyword-difficulty measurement. No GSC/Bing query export or paid keyword dataset was available to this research task. An observed competitor title is evidence of that publisher's targeting, not proof of customer demand or current ranking position. Ownership of the sampled sites was not determined; do not call every site a commercially independent competitor.

## Recommendation

Keep **pikobuy spreadsheet** as the homepage focus; use `/spreadsheet/` for the live browsable catalog. Add a visible **Articles** navigation link to `/articles/`. Make `/guide/`, `/qc/`, `/shipping/` and `/faq/` real independent pages with useful content and links into the article collection. Build ten distinct articles around concrete decisions, rather than ten near-duplicate introductions. Do not add dates or “best” to every headline, and do not imply unmeasured rankings, test purchases, verified sellers, product authenticity or permanent discount offers.

## Search evidence

Queries executed: `pikobuy spreadsheet`; `pikobuy guide shipping qc`; `pikobuy.com help shopping agent`; `site:pikobuy.com protocol shipping returns payment`; `Pikobuy spreadsheet shipping calculator QC size links guide`; `site:pikobuy.to how to order pikobuy returns tracking`; `pikobuy tracking not updating`; `pikobuy return refund 5 days`; `pikobuy link not working weidian`; `pikobuy shipping cost estimate`.

Two search systems were used. The second system produced many unrelated generic shipping results for broad multi-term queries, so the direct official pages and narrowly targeted results provide the usable evidence.

## Official source register

The following concise facts were checked directly. Keep attribution narrow: these pages support their own process or policy statements, not our recommendations about a particular item or seller. Search-tool pages generally carry a 200-word source-derived response budget; do not copy or extensively paraphrase the same official page across ten articles. Write original practical examples and clearly identified editorial methods, with short factual anchors and source links.

- **O1** https://www.pikobuy.com/guide (`turn16view1`, also `turn12search0`, `turn19search4`). The six-stage guide describes selecting a source item and options, searching a link/keywords, a purchase payment, warehouse photo/defect inspection, a separate international shipping payment, and delivery tracking. It lists Taobao, Tmall, 1688, Weidian and Yupoo as sources. It says tracking information is available within three days **after shipment**, not after product payment.
- **O2** https://www.pikobuy.com/home (`turn16view0`, `turn13search2`). Homepage describes searches and submission of links, images or sourcing requests; purchasing from sellers; warehouse inspection/repacking; and selection of shipping options. This supports image-based sourcing at official Pikobuy, not a claim that our preview image-search tool already works.
- **O3** https://www.pikobuy.com/shipping-cost (`turn18view6`, `turn19search0`). Official estimate form asks destination, product type, weight in kg and length/width/height in cm. No fixed rate, universally supported route, delivery guarantee or universal volumetric divisor was verified.
- **O4** https://www.pikobuy.com/protocol/shipping (`turn16view2`). Forwarding terms describe warehouse receipt, unpacking/inspection, inspection photos and optional paid detailed photos. Professional inspection is not offered for special/professional products. Forwarded goods have a different after-sales arrangement: the sender/seller handles after-sales, while Pikobuy can help send goods back. Logistics is provided by third parties.
- **O5** https://www.pikobuy.com/protocol/returns (`turn16view3`). Return eligibility depends on the seller's guarantee, resale condition and warehouse timing. Application window is described as 120 hours counted from the next hour after the status becomes In Warehouse. The page lists exclusions and requires seller consent. Its unconditional-return fee formula includes freight and RMB 5; however, different passages conflict about first-leg freight for originally free-shipping items. Explain this uncertainty and require order-specific confirmation. Do not promise a universal refund outcome or completion date.
- **O6** https://www.pikobuy.com/contact (`turn19search14`, `turn12search15`). Contact result presents a ticket route. Link to the official support page for account-specific questions rather than inventing helpdesk response times.

## Ten distinct article briefs

The proposed keywords below are editorial targets inferred from observed pages and public workflow, **not measured query volumes**. Each article should include an immediately useful answer, a distinct worked example/checklist, links to the relevant live catalog or guide pages, and the short source notes appropriate to its specific claims. “Worked example” means explicitly hypothetical; never present an invented order, measurement, quote or photo set as a real test.

### 1. How to Use a Pikobuy Spreadsheet to Build a Useful Shortlist
- Slug: `/articles/pikobuy-spreadsheet-guide/`
- Primary intent: `pikobuy spreadsheet guide`; supporting: `pikobuy finds`, `pikobuy links`, `how to use pikobuy spreadsheet`.
- Evidence: `turn18view0`, `turn18view1`, `turn12search1`.
- Distinct scope: using this project's search/category/sort/detail interfaces; keep homepage focused on live discovery.
- Suggested sections: choose one category; compare like-for-like variants; separate product ID from catalog record ID; record source price versus final cost; make a three-item shortlist; decide what is still missing.
- Original practical element: a small comparison table of fields, not fabricated products: source ID, variant, size evidence, photo status, unresolved question, next action.
- Link to `/spreadsheet/`, article 3 (links), article 4 (QC), article 6 (shipping).
- Official anchor: O1 only where explaining handoff to the agent. Most of the article should describe our actual interface and original research method.

## Architecture and linking

1. Header: Spreadsheet, Guide, QC, Shipping, FAQ, **Articles**. If space is tight use a proper mobile menu instead of horizontal overflow.
2. Homepage should show only the latest four article cards and a clear link to the full index. Keep live products prominent and preserve the user's short mobile layout preference.
3. Each article belongs to one principal stage (Discover / Order / Inspect / Ship / After-sales), with 2–3 contextual related articles. Avoid identical unrelated footers on all ten articles.
4. FAQ page: at least 24 genuinely different, source-backed questions, grouped by stage; use compact accessible disclosure controls. Link from relevant answers to the detailed article or official source.
5. Locale parity: all existing 24 languages need the same ten article URLs, FAQ count, core facts and functional page navigation. Keep the current article when switching language, set HTML lang correctly, and only declare alternates that actually exist. No English fallback hidden behind a translated heading.
6. Structured data: Article + BreadcrumbList for articles, collection/breadcrumb for the index. FAQPage markup must mirror visible FAQ content. Do not promise FAQ rich results. Preview remains noindex until formal-domain approval.
7. All product/image/QC data remains FindSpreadsheet-sourced. Article examples should use text tables or existing live product data, with no fabricated catalog or generated product images.

## Claims to reject unless separately verified

- Fixed shipping prices, universal shipping times, cheapest/best route, tax-free outcomes, permanent customs thresholds or fixed volumetric divisor.
- “100% safe”, “verified authentic”, “seller verified”, a completed personal order or hands-on review that did not occur.
- Free returns in all cases, guaranteed refund timing, seller consent assumed, free unlimited warehouse storage.
- Competitor coupon codes, weekly discount percentages, fixed free QC photo counts or per-photo fees not in the verified official page.
- A functioning QC/image-search endpoint on our site while upstream integrations are unavailable.
- Keyword search volumes or traffic potential expressed as measured facts; publication count presented as ranking strength.
